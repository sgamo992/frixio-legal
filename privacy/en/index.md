# Frixio privacy policy

*Last updated: November 11, 2026* · [Versione italiana](../)

Frixio is an Android app that slows down the opening of the apps you choose (for example social media) with a small friction: a breath, a question or a calculation. This page explains which data it uses and where that data goes.

**In short: Frixio does not collect any data. Everything the app stores stays on your phone, and the app does not even have permission to access the Internet.**

## Who publishes the app

Frixio is published by Guido Giordano, independent developer.
Contact: [frixio.info@gmail.com](mailto:frixio.info@gmail.com)

## The accessibility service

To know when you are opening an app you chose to slow down, Frixio uses Android's accessibility service. It uses it for one thing only: knowing **which app is in the foreground**, that is its technical name (for example `com.instagram.android`).

Frixio **does not read** screen content, texts, messages, passwords or what you type, and it does not perform actions on your behalf. The service is configured so that it cannot access window content.

You can turn the service off at any time from **Android Settings → Accessibility**. Frixio immediately stops slowing down apps.

## What Frixio stores, on your phone only

- the apps you chose to protect and the friction type chosen for each one;
- the app's settings (for example whether you completed the initial setup or whether a pause is active);
- the friction history: when a friction appeared, for which app and how it went (you continued or you let it go). It is only used to show you statistics inside the app.

This data stays in your phone's storage. Frixio does not send it to any server, does not include it in Google backups and does not share it with anyone.

If you switch phones using Android's direct phone-to-phone transfer, this data may be copied to the new phone together with your other apps. It does not go through Frixio or the developer.

## No data sent

Frixio does not have permission to access the Internet, so it cannot send data. It contains no advertising, no analytics tools and no third-party libraries that collect data.

Google Play, as for every app installed from the store, may collect installation and crash statistics according to your Google account settings and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Frixio adds nothing to this data.

## "Report a problem"

From the app you can report a problem by email. Frixio prepares an email in your email app: you see it, you can edit or delete any part of it, and you decide whether to send it. Frixio never sends anything on its own.

At the bottom of the email there is a summary of the phone's state that helps understand the problem:

- Frixio version, phone brand, model and Android version;
- whether the accessibility service and the battery optimization exclusion are active;
- **how many** apps are protected or suspended, and whether a pause is active (not **which** apps);
- how many times each friction type appeared and how it went in the last 7 days, and when the last one appeared.

If you send the email, the developer receives your email address and the text you sent. They are only used to reply and to understand the problem, are not shared with anyone and are kept only as long as needed to handle the report.

## Deleting your data

You can delete all of Frixio's data in two ways:

- **Android Settings → Apps → Frixio → Storage → Clear data**;
- uninstalling the app.

If you sent a report and want it deleted, write to [frixio.info@gmail.com](mailto:frixio.info@gmail.com).

## Children

Frixio is not intended for children under 13 and does not collect anyone's data, including theirs.

## Changes

If Frixio changes the way it uses data (for example if one day it needs Internet access), this page will be updated **before** the new version of the app is available, and the date at the top will change.
